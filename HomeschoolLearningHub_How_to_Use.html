How to Use Homeschool Learning Hub

An interactive homeschool system for organizing students, curriculum, weekly plans, daily learning records, grades, and progress.

Start Here: The Homeschool Flow

Use the app in this order. Each section has a different job, so your information stays organized instead of being entered in multiple places.

👨‍👩‍👧 Students→ 📚 Curriculum→ 📅 Planner→ 📝 Daily Log→ 📈 Grades→ 🎯 Progress

1. Students — Who are you homeschooling?

1

Add each student

Open Students and add the child’s name, grade level, age, and start date.

Tip: Set up your students first. The other areas use the student selection to keep records separated correctly.

2. Curriculum — What are they studying?

2

Create subjects

Add the subject, student, curriculum or materials, total lessons, and learning objectives.

Curriculum completion and academic grades are intentionally separate. Completing more lessons does not automatically mean a higher grade.

3. Planner — What do you plan to teach?

3

Plan the week

Choose a student, move through the week, and plan lessons with a date, subject, activity, duration, and notes.

Important: The Planner represents your intention. It is not your official daily record.

4. Daily Log — What actually happened?

4

Record the learning

After the lesson, use Daily Log to record the date, student, subject, activity, actual duration, and notes.

This is the section that turns your plan into your homeschool record. Learning days, instruction hours, logged activities, and curriculum progress are based on the learning records.

5. Grades — How did the student perform?

5

Record assessments

Add homework, quizzes, tests, or projects with a score from 0–100.

Use Grade Weights to decide how Homework, Quiz, Test, and Project scores contribute to the weighted average. The weights must total 100%.

6. Progress — How are we doing overall?

6

Review the complete picture

Select a student to see learning days, instruction hours, activities logged, curriculum completion, and academic performance.

Use Print Report when you want a printable progress summary.

Dashboard — Your quick overview

The Dashboard brings the important numbers together: students, learning days, instruction hours, overall grade, weekly planned/completed lessons, and curriculum progress.

Quick Actions let you jump directly into the most common tasks without changing tabs first.

Backup & Restore

✓

Protect your records

Use Backup to save your current data as a JSON file. Use Restore to bring that backup back into the app.

Recommendation: Keep a backup somewhere safe before changing devices or clearing browser data.

Print

Use the Print button in the header for a printable version of the current app view, or use Print Report from Progress for a student-focused report.

Quick Example

Sarah studies Mathematics using Saxon Math 5/4.

You add Sarah under Students → add Mathematics under Curriculum → plan “Fractions — Lesson 12” under Planner → after teaching it, record “Fractions — Lesson 12” in Daily Log → enter the quiz score under Grades → review the result under Progress.

You're Ready

Set up your students first, then work through the flow one section at a time. The app is designed so you do not have to enter the same information in every section.
